# Section Insurance Verification & EHR Management 

- **The Clinical Note Sync:** The dentist forgets to sign a note, delaying the claim.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I monitor the Dentrix "Unattached Procedures" report daily, prompting the clinician to sign notes so I can attach the required clinical data and submit claims without delay.
        
- **Digital Document Management:** A patient’s physical referral needs to be accessible remotely.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I utilize the Dentrix Document Center to categorize and upload the referral, ensuring the clinical team and I can access the same data simultaneously from any location.
        
- **The "Double-Booked" Ledger:** A patient was checked out twice, creating a duplicate entry.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I audit the Ledger’s "Transaction Summary" to identify the duplicate, then void the incorrect entry and reconcile the day’s production to ensure the office's reporting is accurate.

- **The "Non-Duplication" Clause:** Both plans have a "Non-Duplication" clause for a crown.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I explain to the patient that the secondary plan won't pay if the primary paid more than the secondary’s allowance, ensuring they understand their true out-of-pocket costs.
        
- **The Birthday Rule Dispute:** Parents are divorced and both carry insurance for a child.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I identify the primary carrier by the parent whose birthday falls earlier in the year, then verify any court-ordered "Custody Decree" to ensure correct billing order.
        
- **Medicaid as Secondary:** A patient has private insurance and Medicaid.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I always bill the private carrier first as the "Payor of Last Resort," then submit the EOB to Medicaid to cover any remaining allowable patient responsibility.

# Section Claims Processing & Accounts Receivable (AR) 

- **The "Low-Hanging Fruit":** The AR report shows many small balances under $20.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I use Dentrix "Quick Letters" to send a batch of "Small Balance" notices, efficiently clearing the report of minor debts while I focus on larger insurance claims.
        
- **Timely Filing Deadline:** A group of claims is approaching the 90-day filing limit.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I prioritize these "At-Risk" claims above all other tasks, calling the payers daily to ensure they are on file before the legal deadline for reimbursement expires.
        
- **The High-Value Surgery AR:** An implant claim worth $2,000 is 45 days past due.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I contact the "Provider Relations" line to confirm receipt of the X-ray, then request a supervisor review to expedite the payment for this high-production procedure immediately.

- **The "Frequency Limitation" Denial:** Insurance denies a prophy because it was done 5 months ago.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I review the "Patient History" in Dentrix to find the previous date, then appeal by highlighting a "medical necessity" narrative if the patient has periodontal history.
        
- **The Bulk EOB Discrepancy:** A bulk check arrives, but one patient’s payment is missing.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I post the payments for the correct patients, then immediately call the payer to find the "missing" payment status, preventing the ledger from remaining inaccurately open.
        
- **The Overpayment Scenario:** An insurance company pays more than the estimated amount.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I verify if the patient has an upcoming balance; if not, I process a refund through the Ledger to ensure the office doesn't hold unclaimed credits.

- **The "Missing Information" Rejection:** A claim is rejected because the "Missing Tooth Clause" wasn't addressed.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I update the Dentrix Chart with the date of extraction, then resubmit the claim with the "Initial Placement" box checked to ensure the insurance carrier processes it.
        
- **Narrative Requirements:** A claim for a "Build-up" (D2950) is stalled.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I draft a specific narrative stating "less than 50% of tooth structure remains," then attach the intraoral photo and X-ray to prove the necessity of the procedure.
        
- **Electronic Attachment Failure:** The clearinghouse says the X-ray didn't transmit.
    
- **Ideal Action:** I verify the attachment ID in the NEA FastAttach portal and re-link it to the Dentrix claim, ensuring the payer receives all documentation needed for immediate adjudication.
